# OOM Mesh Quality Investigation

**Date**: 2026-04-21
**Question**: Are the 24.3% OOM meshes (1571/6494 attempted at res=512) low-quality garbage,
or legitimately complex geometry (multi-part, dense, open-surface) that is valuable
for training?

**Method**: Stratified sampling from existing precompute logs (`logs/precompute_feat18_r512_g8/rank*.log`)
and OOM sentinels (`feat18_512/failed_rank*.txt`), CPU-side trimesh analysis of structural
properties on each sample, per-bucket aggregation.

Buckets (samples drawn pseudo-randomly within each band):
- `oom_small`:  OOM request 3–30 GiB   (first-order overflow)
- `oom_medium`: OOM request 30–200 GiB (serious overflow)
- `oom_huge`:   OOM request ≥ 200 GiB  (pathological, some > 900 GiB)
- `ok_fast`:    succeeded, enc 3–10 s   (median meshes)
- `ok_slow`:    succeeded, enc 100–300 s (tail but no OOM)

---

## Key question

Is `max_tri_per_voxel` (P_MAX driver) large for OOM meshes because of:
  (a) **Genuine dense detail** — fabric, chainmail, dense foliage, concentric shells
      → high-quality, we want to keep them.
  (b) **Pathological structure** — overlapping duplicate faces, z-fighting inner copies,
      tiny floating components, corrupted topology
      → low-quality, safe to drop.

Both produce the same symptom but imply different optimization strategies.

### Hypothesis to confirm from data

Signals that would point toward **(a) legitimate complex geometry**:
- High `n_scene_geoms` (multi-part glb with meaningful parts, not single-face fragments)
- Moderate-to-high `n_components` but each component has non-trivial face count
- Low `n_deg_faces` / `n_dup_faces` (mesh is clean, just complex)
- Reasonable `bbox_diag` and `aspect_ratio` (not degenerate to line/plane)
- High `tri_density` but with `surface_area` also high (real surface detail)

Signals that would point toward **(b) low-quality junk**:
- Very low `bbox_diag` or extreme `aspect_ratio` (mesh collapsed to line/plane)
- Many components but most are tiny (few faces each) → dust particles
- High `n_deg_faces` or `n_dup_faces` before normalization → broken GLB export
- `tri_density` anomalously high despite tiny `surface_area` → overlapping/duplicated geometry
